Colours mean something · always with a word

ClosedNormal and safe: closed, locked, armed. A discreet green word and dot, never a green block.
OpenTo look at: open, moving, battery low.
OnOn or running: a light, the heating. Gold, like the lit tiles.
AlarmOnly an alarm or a failure. Red everywhere else teaches people to ignore it.

Principles

A remote, not a dashboardThe home screen opens pages and fills one screen, no more. One page per thing. Upkeep (batteries, silent sensors) goes elsewhere.
A living backgroundKeep the theme’s halos (--mk-bg-art): glass needs light behind it. A flat colour makes every tile look dead.
One featured tile, then sizesThe thing most reached for is wide and gold, first. Others vary: a tall tile for a big number, regular ones, a wide one to close the grid.
A hue per place, gold for the starA discreet tint per place or kind of device (.mk-tint-*) tells tiles apart. One hue per tile, never more.
Line icons, never emojiEmoji bring their own colours and styles. Use the shipped icons (icons.svg, icon()).
Open: never by accidentActions that open, unlock or start sit together on the first row and are held to confirm. Never from the home screen.
Close: never forgottenAlone on its row, one tap. While something is open it becomes the gold button, and the home tile turns warm with a Close shortcut.
Buttons never moveOpen or closed, each button keeps its place; only its emphasis changes. The thumb learns where to tap.
Old values are dimmed, not banneredThe Miakapp bar already says “Connecting…”. Your app only dims the values that may be old and disables the controls. Never a banner of your own, never a fake “up to date”.
An app, not a web pagePages slide in and have a history entry each; a swipe from the left edge goes back. Text is not selectable, except in fields.
