Principles

A remote, not a dashboardThe home screen opens pages and fits one screen. One page per thing. Upkeep (batteries, silent sensors) goes elsewhere.
Open, see, doA tile opens. A page shows the state first, big, then large action buttons, then the rare ones apart. Never mixed.
Room or device type, never both“Bedrooms” or “Shutters”, not a rooms section next to a temperatures section repeating them. Words residents use.
What is on glowsOne strong signal: the golden glow of something that is on. Everything else stays calm.
One gradient per screenThe gradient is a signature: one title or one key number, not everywhere.
Say when the home goes quietDim stale values, show an honest banner, disable controls. Never a fake “up to date”.
Big fingers, little text44 px targets at least, tabular digits, three text sizes per screen.
Free to change everythingThe tokens are a starting point. Make the home’s own theme with its residents.
